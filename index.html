2Decision · what can change

vt pounds reserved for visitors on day t
et pounds harvested by employees on day t

The two sliders set the same daily targets for each day. Choose weather by day to adjust visitor demand; prices, costs, and ripe supply stay fixed.

3Constraints · what limits the plan

vt + et ≤ AtApples used cannot exceed ripe supply.
0 ≤ vt ≤ Dv,tVisitor pounds cannot exceed weather-adjusted daily demand.
0 ≤ et ≤ min(Ds,t, Lt, St)Employee pounds cannot exceed store demand, labor, or storage.

Why optimization?

Visitor picking can bring revenue but leaves apples exposed to spoilage or being imperfect. Employee harvest supplies store sales but uses labor and storage. Demand and weather change across days. Optimization finds the daily allocation with the highest total expected profit while respecting the limits.
